## General

***RPGDungeonCrawler*** is a game project that aims to take the player into a 3D yet retro roleplaying environment, that will combine traditional RPG elements with the modern day Large Language Models to deliver a infinitely replayable experience.

## Character Creation

After the player starts the game, they will be taken to a character creation screen. Here the player can name their character and choose from three different classes (so far).
- Soldier: Favours survivability and damage
- Rogue: Favours speed and hit chance
- Sorcerer: Favours magic ability and dabbles a little bit in several areas

These difference are determined by the player attributes, which are preselected based on the selected class. However, levelling up is a planned feature where you can upgrade your attributes. The game uses tooltips in the character creation so hover your mouse over them to learn more about each:
- Constitution
- Strength
- Dexterity
- Intelligence
- Charisma
- Speed

You can also choose the player's sex. Based on your chosen class and sex, you will get a player portrait to represent your character's looks.

## Town

After you create your character, you will spawn in a medieval town at night, also reffered to as the game hub in the `Gameplay Loop` section of this manual. Here you will get two locations, the town inn and the merchant's shop. Currenlty, there is no indication of these locations but they can be easily found. The inn is the biggest building in the centre of the street and the merchant's shop is just across it to the left. Approaching their doors will prompot you to open them (See `Controls`). You can approach the inn and enter it which will take you to the inn screen. Here you can click on the `Spend the night` option to change the day to the next one. This will spawn you at the town in daytime. You can then access the merchant or head straight to the town exit to explore a new dungeon.

## Merchant

After you enter the merchant shop, you can press the `Show me your wares` button to get to the buying/selling screen. Here you can either click on your inventory to sell something or click on the merchant's inventory to buy something. After you are done, click the `[Leave]` button to leave.

## Inventory

At any point of the game except when you are in the inn or the merchant's shop, you can access your inventory (see `Controls`). Your inventory items will be stored in 24 slots. These are filled up according to the first blank space. Hover over any item to see it's stats. Any equipment item (armor or weapon) may be equipped in it's approapriate slots on the right if you click on them.

## Gameplay Loop

This consists of features (levelling up) which are being worked and are not available in the current build.
- Player spawns in town at night, replenishes health and mana by sleeping at the inn and sells loot or buys new equipment for more adventure.
- If the player has enough experience, they can also level up at the inn and get stronger to face bigger challenges
- At day the player heads out to a new procedurally generated dungeons
- At the dungeon the player fights new monsters and finds new loot as well gaining experience points
- Player leaves dungeon to go back to the town
- Repeat

## Loot

The `loot` mentioned in the loop updates according to player level. At higher levels, the merchant offers better equipment and the player finds better loot. According to the player class, different loot will suit the player better. 
- Light armor for rogues and sorceres
- heavy armor for soldiers
- Daggers for rogues
- Staffs for sorcerers
- Swords for soldeirs

## Controls

- WASD keys for movement
- E to `interact` and open doors
- spacebar to jump
- shift to sprint
- i to open and close inventory
- Esc to open pause menu (only resume button works currently)

## Installation
To run the project in Godot, you should do the following:
- Download a dot NET version of Godot 4.4-stable: https://godotengine.org/download/archive/4.4-stable
- Extract the downloaded package
- Run the Godot_v4.4-stable_mono_win64 file.
- Click on import button on the upper left part of the window
- Select the project directory
- After the project opens completely, click on the "run project" button on the upper right part of the window or press `F5`.
